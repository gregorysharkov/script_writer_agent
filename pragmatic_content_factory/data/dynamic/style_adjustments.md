# Style Adjustments Log

A chronological log of tone and style changes based on feedback and evolving brand voice.

## Current Style Guidelines

### Tone: "Confident Pragmatist"

- Speak from experience, not theory
- Use concrete examples over abstract concepts
- Acknowledge trade-offs honestly
- Avoid overselling or underselling

### Voice Characteristics

1. **Technical but Accessible**: Mix jargon with plain explanations
2. **Direct**: Get to the point, respect reader's time
3. **Evidence-based**: Back claims with data or experience
4. **Self-aware**: Acknowledge limitations and biases

### Sentence Structure

- Prefer active voice
- Keep sentences concise
- Use bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- One idea per paragraph
